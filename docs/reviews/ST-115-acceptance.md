## Remarks
1. Scenario 1's „Angehängt an“ lists all four: the test asserts the read model's four entries, the retired flag and the page's „LG-041 · Ausgemustert“; the other three lines come from the shared `attachedToText` (ST-114). Acceptable.
2. Scenario 2 does not assert the group header („Flipper · EM …“) although it asserts the two parts, closed folds and the order. The grouping itself is covered by `attachment-choice.integration.test.ts` (groups by machine category/technology) and by the shared `GroupedChoice`, whose folding/„Alle auswählen“ the story note leaves to ST-094. Acceptable.
3. Scenario 11's post-conflict page is asserted as a fresh load. In the browser the rejection shows the reason in the client component and the list becomes current on the reload the reason text asks for; the scenario's „shown again … listing LG-042 and LG-050“ is met after that reload. Acceptable.
4. Scenario 7's "counts on the form „KI“" is proven through `aiIndexStateOfFile` and the details section, which the settings overview derives from the same state. Acceptable.
5. The `DetachFileButton` renders its rejection below the button; G8 asks for above. Minor; the reason is still at the tapped entry.
6. The confirmations after attaching/detaching are computed from `?attached=1` / `?detached=<target>` plus the current state, without `lastCommandBy` and the viewer check of `docs/engineering/conventions.md`, *A confirmation after an action*. The story's no-op requirement (G33, scenario 5) is exactly why a journal check cannot cover it; the convention needs the exception. This is a documentation/approval gap, not a behaviour defect – the resulting state is true and the no-op case is what the story asks for.

Verdict: **accepted with remarks**
