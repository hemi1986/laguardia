# Code review – ST-086: Helper dashboard – my claimed defects and what changed on them
Base: main · Commits: 13 · Date: 2026-10-24

Checked and found sound (no finding): module boundaries (the page composes Repair's `changesOnClaimedDefects`, Collection's `machineLabels` and Team's `teamMemberNames` through their `index.ts`, ADR 0002, conventions "the page composes"). The journal is read only typed, through `readJournal` and `repairEvents` (story notes, conventions "The event journal"). Time goes through `hoursBefore` / `elapsedHours` and the injected clock. No `new Date()`. Texts come from the catalogue in the G25 wording. `check-language.ts` reports no new warning. All 22 tests of the two helper dashboard files pass.

Fold correctness, verified against the commands. Entries are folded in `position` order. A claim by her resets the changes. A takeover is told apart from an assignment by `assignedBy`, and a technician assigning the defect to himself counts as a takeover. Her own release or resolution ends the claim without a line, so the defect is not listed. Closing on retirement ends it without a line. The mark-removal release is detected by `releasedClaimOf === her` plus the same point in time. That cannot misfire: a separate earlier release leaves no claim to release, so `releasedClaimOf` is empty then, and one command shares one `clock.now()` (conventions, command layer point 3). The 168 h window is applied both in the journal filter and to `leftAt`.

## Resolution (implementer, 2026-10-24)
- **1 (major) – fixed:** "starts the lines again when she claims a defect back that left her" – mutation-checked: deleting `leftAt = undefined` or `changes = []` now fails it.
- **2 – fixed:** tests for a new title in the step that removes the mark (above the release line) and for closing on retirement (no longer listed).
- **3 – skipped:** the module needs her current claims to know which journals to fold; letting the page pass them in would move a Repair rule into the page. Revisit if the dashboard gets slow.
- **4 – skipped:** fine at museum scale (lead dev's own estimate); `formerClaimant` on `EVT-DefectResolved` would be an `events.yaml` change – revisit when it matters.
- **5 – fixed:** `elapsedInWords(hours)` in `src/platform/messages/team/shared.ts`, used by the triage list's „wartet seit …“ and the change lines' „vor …“.
- **6 – skipped:** the `</li></ul>` and the regex assert that no further line follows – what scenarios 5 and 13 say; a markup change touches them knowingly.
- **7 – fixed:** `WithoutTime` instead of `DistributiveNamed`; `DefectsSection` takes `ClaimedByYouItem | OpenDefectsItem`.
