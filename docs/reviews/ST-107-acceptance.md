## Verdict

**not accepted.**

The ten scenarios exist, are titled exactly, are at the right seams and pass, and the request form, caps, scope and reference filtering are solid. The following block acceptance:

1. **`ai-assessment-work.ts:209-218`** – `unique()` collapses distinct text passages of one file/page, so "the 8 best text passages" a search takes are not what the chat model is sent. Contradicts the story's Context; no test.
2. **HS-31 at the same moment** – the unique index is there, but its violation is not turned into `ai-assessment-being-written`, and there is no test for the concurrency rule the story names ("rejected until it is written or failed").
3. **Definition of done 2** – the new chat adapter (`openrouter.ts:212-274`) has no test at the named OpenRouter-adapter seam; the zero-data-retention provider rule and the failure mapping of the first request carrying visitors' photos are unverified.
4. **Scenario 1 / G35 / G2a** – no browser or no-JavaScript test (the story review and the story's Notes promise one); only a `http-equiv="refresh"` string is asserted, and "until the AI assessment is written" is not.
5. **Minor, fix alongside:** scenarios 8 and 10 do not assert "records nothing"; scenario 3 does not assert the model's category/technology; the 10 newest photos are only counted; the note is kept only in the runner state, not in the rendered form.
