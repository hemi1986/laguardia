A new migration rather than editing 0008: the preview has applied it. **Revisit in ST-039:** the system's retirement policy has no TeamMemberId for "triaged by" – that story decides how a system triage is stored (it will meet the CHECK).
- Major 2 – fixed: the 72-hour rule and the waiting hours live in Repair (`triageList(db, clock)`, `problemReportForTriage(db, clock, id)`); the page only adds names.
- Minors: `shown` → `withNames` (fixed); unused `outcome` of `triageForTest` (removed); helper e2e vacuous until ST-018/020/022 (by decision – noted in those stories); titled page test does not click from the list (skipped – the untitled e2e does); "unbekannt" from the machine record's section (skipped – one wording for an unknown team member everywhere); missing machine label `??
