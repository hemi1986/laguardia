## Edge cases not covered by the story

## Verdict
**Not accepted** – 9 of 10 scenarios are genuinely and correctly tested and pass, and the automated suite (unit/integration, e2e, CI on both required checks) is green. However, one context rule central to this story is violated in realistic use: **"Sessions expire after 90 days without use"** does not hold for team members who only view team pages (the far more common pattern than submitting a form every day) – their session cookie silently expires 90 days after **login**, not after 90 days of **inactivity**, because Better Auth cannot re-issue the cookie's `Max-Age` from a Server Component render and swallows the resulting error. This is independently reproduced (not just taken from the code review) via Better Auth's and Next.js's own source, and the scenario test cannot catch it because it bypasses real cookie semantics. Together with the concurrent-throttling bypass and the unvalidated setup username (both already identified as fixable within this story, per `docs/reviews/ST-004-code-review.md`), these should be fixed in the refactor step before this story is accepted; none of them warrant a new story (all are same-story rule violations, fixable well within the story's remaining scope, and no other story owns team login).

## Resolution (main session, 2026-09-27)
The three rule violations were fixed in this story before the pull request (same findings as `ST-004-code-review.md` #1, #3, #4):
- **Cookie sliding:** `src/proxy.ts` renews the session cookie with a fresh 90 days on every page request (`renewedSessionCookie`); test "keeps a team member logged in while she uses La Guardia – 90 days counted from the last use" (use on day 60, logged in on day 120) and a test of the renewed cookie's attributes.
- **Throttling race:** attempts are reserved as failures under a per-username advisory lock; 15 parallel wrong attempts → exactly 10 failed, 5 locked.
- **Setup username:** refuses an empty name and usernames Better Auth would not accept at login.
- Remarks also addressed: the FK test expects `23503`; "or changed" is checked; failed logins are deleted after 30 minutes; the throttling test comment is corrected.
