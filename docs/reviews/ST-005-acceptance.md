### "Deactivating an account ends its sessions" – is the problem-report stand-in honest?
The story explicitly says the work log doesn't exist yet and a problem report stands in – the test follows that instruction. What it actually proves: (a) the journal keeps Anna's `teamMemberId` unchanged after deactivation, and (b) `teamMemberAccounts` still returns her name and username, only flipped to `active: false` (accounts are never deleted, per Out of Scope). Together these two facts are the real precondition for "still shows her name" once a display feature (work log, problem-report list) exists later and resolves `teamMemberId` → name via a join. So the substitute is honest about what it can prove today. What it does **not** prove is that any *rendered* text still shows "Anna Berger" next to something she wrote, because nothing in the current codebase renders a reporter's name yet. That gap is a consequence of the missing work log, not a flaw in this test – but it's worth flagging so whoever builds the work log/problem-report display (ST-020 ff.) remembers to verify names of deactivated members render, not just IDs.

## Verdict
**Accepted with remarks.**

All 11 scenarios have a test with the exact required title, and each test genuinely exercises the Given/When/Then of its scenario through the real module entry point with real assertions (not recomputed values) – including the two hardest ones, the true concurrent double-demotion test and the live role re-check without re-login. `npm run verify` is green, traceability is 11/11, and no personal data is logged.

The one remark that should be closed before merging, or explicitly accepted as a known gap: "Helpers cannot manage accounts" is proven at the module seam but not through the actual page/Server Action path with a real helper session – the page-level guard (`requireTechnician`) exists and reads correctly, but is currently untested. Everything else in this table (concurrent username creation, resets against a non-existent account) is a genuine open question, not a defect, and low-risk enough to leave as-is or fold into ST-069/future stories at the team's discretion.

I could not verify anything on the preview deployment: it is behind Vercel SSO protection (302 redirect to `vercel.com/sso-api`) that I have no bypass secret for, and separately the preview database has no team member account before ST-068 (as the story's own `e2e/team-accounts.spec.ts` documents), so UI login there would not have worked anyway.
